About Yayasan Peduli Amal Indonesia

Founded in 2015, Yayasan Peduli Amal Indonesia has been dedicated to creating positive change in communities worldwide. We believe that every person deserves access to basic necessities and opportunities for a better future.

Our mission is to empower individuals and communities through education, healthcare, housing, and food security programs. With the support of generous donors and dedicated volunteers, we've impacted over 50,000 lives across 25 countries.
